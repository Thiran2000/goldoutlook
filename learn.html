The basics

What is a sovereign?

In Sri Lanka a sovereign (pawn, pavun) is a weight of 8 grams, and most shops quote their price per sovereign. But 22K gold is not all gold, so a 22K sovereign holds a little less than 8 grams of pure gold.

8 gWeight of one sovereign, the unit shops quote.
7.33 gPure gold inside a 22K sovereign (22 parts in 24).
6 gPure gold inside an 18K sovereign (18 parts in 24).
